Services

Commercial real estate expertise across five property types.

Whether you’re buying, selling, leasing, or just weighing your options, Waner Real Estate brings the same full evaluation to every engagement.

Office

Representation for tenants seeking new space, owners leasing up a building, and investors evaluating office assets across Denver’s submarkets. Lease negotiation, renewal strategy, and disposition are all handled with the same close attention to your bottom line.

Medical

Medical and healthcare-adjacent practices have specific needs — visibility, access, build-out requirements, and long lease horizons. Waner Real Estate helps practices find the right site and negotiate terms that protect the practice for years to come.

Retail

From single storefronts to multi-tenant centers, retail success is about the right location and the right lease structure. Support covers site selection, tenant mix strategy, and sale or lease-up of retail assets.

Land

Land transactions carry unique risk around entitlements, zoning, and use. Waner Real Estate approaches land sales and acquisitions with a clear-eyed read on what a parcel can actually support.

Mixed-Use

Mixed-use projects layer together multiple property types and stakeholders. Coordination across uses, phasing, and financing considerations helps keep these more complex deals on track.

Advisory

Not every situation calls for a transaction. Waner Real Estate offers independent advisory on valuation, market timing, and whether buying, selling, or leasing actually serves your goals right now.
